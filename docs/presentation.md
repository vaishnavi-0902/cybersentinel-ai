# CyberSentinel Presentation

## Slide 1: Title
CyberSentinel AI

## Slide 2: Introduction
AI-based defensive cybersecurity monitoring for suspicious activity and anomaly detection.

## Slide 3: Problem Statement
Modern systems produce large volumes of events. Manual monitoring is slow and error-prone.

## Slide 4: Proposed System
A platform that combines AI, rules, and dashboards for safer activity analysis.

## Slide 5: Objectives
Detect suspicious events, store alerts, train ML models, and present results to analysts.

## Slide 6: Architecture
UI -> API -> ML -> Database.

## Slide 7: ML Methodology
Uses Random Forest and Isolation Forest for classification and anomaly detection.

## Slide 8: Risk Scoring
Assessment based on model confidence and rule-based detections.

## Slide 9: Dashboard
Security metrics, charts, and event timeline.

## Slide 10: Deployment
Docker, Docker Compose, Kubernetes, and CI/CD.

## Slide 11: Results
High model performance on simulated defensive datasets.

## Slide 12: Future Scope
Threat intelligence integration and richer alert workflows.
